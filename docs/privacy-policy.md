# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Safesight (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Safesight iOS app.

## 1. Summary

Scan photos used for analysis are sent to our Safesight API so a vision model can return structured results. Profile data, scan history, and gallery images are stored on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. Information we process

- Profile details you enter (name, dwelling type, focus areas, look-hardness settings)
- Photos you capture or upload for a scan, plus analysis results
- Subscription status via Apple and RevenueCat
- Basic technical data needed to operate the API (such as IP address for rate limiting)

## 3. How we use information

- To analyze rooms and show hazards, scores, and recommendations
- To enforce free-tier limits and Premium entitlements
- To operate, secure, and debug the API
- To improve product quality using aggregated or de-identified operational signals where feasible

## 4. Where analysis runs

When you scan, the image and focus metadata are transmitted to the Safesight API. The API calls Google Gemini to generate structured JSON. Do not scan images you are not allowed to share with these processors.

## 5. On-device storage

Scan images and history are stored locally. Unstarred scans may be purged after a retention period. Logging out can erase local Safesight data on that device.

## 6. Third parties

- **Apple** — distribution and In-App Purchases  
- **RevenueCat** — subscription status  
- **Google Gemini** (via our API) — vision analysis  
- **Product destinations** (e.g. Amazon) if you open recommendations  

Their policies apply when you use those services.

## 7. Your choices

Limit photos you upload; delete local history via log out; manage Premium in Apple / RevenueCat settings; decline camera or photo permissions (capture/upload will not work).

## 8. Children

Safesight is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe we processed a child’s data inappropriately.

## 9. Security

API calls use HTTPS. Production secrets are kept out of public source control. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
